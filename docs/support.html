Questions

How do I play?
From the title screen, tap How to play. It takes 7 steps, about a minute.
My run is gone. Can I get it back?
The game saves as you play. If you quit mid-run, the title screen shows CONTINUE. Deleting the app deletes the save; there's no cloud copy.
The text is too small (or too big).
Settings, then Text size: Standard, Large or Larger.
The motion is too much.
Settings, then Reduce Motion. Shake, particles, slides and bounces stop; screens cross-fade instead.
Can I turn the sound off?
Settings, then Sound. Volume is next to it.
The count is too slow.
Settings, then Count speed. You can also tap during a count to skip to the end.
Does it need the internet?
No. It plays fully offline, with no account and no ads.
What data does it collect?
None. See the privacy policy.
