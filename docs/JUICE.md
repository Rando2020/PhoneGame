# Juice pass — animations, pixel art, 16-bit audio

`meldlings.html` → **PLAY · cribbage**. 205 KB, still one file, still no server.

## 16-bit audio (all synthesised, zero bytes)

`web/audio.js` is a real WebAudio synth rather than beeps:

- **Voices**: pulse / triangle / saw / sine with **detune pairs** for width, optional
  vibrato, pitch slides, and a shared **delay send** — that combination is what
  reads as SNES-era rather than NES-era
- **Percussion**: sine-sweep kick, noise+tone snare, filtered-noise hat
- **Limiter** on the master bus so stacked cascade hits never clip
- **Music sequencer**: a small tracker with lead / bass / arp / pad / drum rows,
  scheduled against the audio clock with lookahead so it doesn't drift.
  Three loops — `menu` (82 BPM, A minor), `run` (126 BPM, D minor),
  `boss` (150 BPM, E phrygian). Boss rounds swap the track automatically.
- **Ducking**: music dips under every multiplier slam

Nothing is embedded, so the OGG files from the Godot build aren't needed here.

## The cascade, rebuilt

This is where the dopamine had to land, so it got the most attention:

1. **The counted hand stays on screen.** As each sub-score is announced, the exact
   cards that produced it **light up** in that category's colour and lift — gold for
   fifteens, blue for pairs, green for runs, red for flush, purple for nobs. The
   starter sits apart with a gold ring.
2. **Pixel category icons** on every cascade row (`15`, two cards, ascending bars,
   a suit fan, a J) so each line has a visual identity.
3. **The number counts up** rather than snapping, eased, with a **rising pentatonic
   tick** per event. The pitch climbs as the count climbs, so a big hand literally
   plays an ascending arpeggio. Each category has its own root note.
4. **The multiplier slams.** `base × mult` fades in, then lands with a four-note
   chord, kick, a low noise sweep, **screen shake scaled to the score**, and a
   **pip burst** whose particle count scales too.
5. **Target crossed mid-round** fires an immediate "TARGET MET" with its own sting
   and a bigger burst, rather than being discovered silently at round end.
6. **Coaching line** still lands after the slam — what the best keep would have scored.

Verified event shape (a real committed deal):

```
fifteen  Fifteen 2   +2   Q♦,5♦
fifteen  Fifteen 4   +2   Q♣,5♦
fifteen  Fifteen 6   +2   Q♦,5♣
fifteen  Fifteen 8   +2   Q♣,5♣
pair     Pair of 5s  +2   5♦,5♣
pair     Pair of Qs  +2   Q♦,Q♣
levels   +24
36 base × 2.03 = 73    [Facet · Counting House]
```

Six ticks, then a level bonus, then the slam. That's the rhythm.

## Animations

- **Deal-in**: the six cards fly in from off-screen, staggered 70 ms, each with its
  own card sound
- **Starter flip**: rotates in on the Y axis with a cut sound; recuts re-trigger it
- **Card select**: lifts with a gold glow; unkept cards dim so the crib-bound cards
  are always readable at a glance
- **Number pop** on every tick, bigger pop on the slam
- **Shop purchases** burst pips

## New assets

`assets/icons/` — five pixel category icons (14×9) and four particle pips,
generated by `tools/make_icons.py`. Same palette as the cards and creatures.

## What I'd still add next

In rough order of dopamine per unit of work:

1. **Creature reactions during the cascade.** The sprites and animations exist
   (`idle`/`attack`/`hurt`) but the cribbage screens don't show the Meldling during
   a count. Having it react on the slam is cheap and would land well.
2. **Score-tier feedback.** A hand scoring 3× the average should feel different from
   one scoring average — different chord, brighter burst, a word on screen
   ("SOLID" / "HUGE" / "TWENTY-NINE").
3. **The 29.** A perfect hand deserves a unique celebration; right now it's just a
   big number.
4. **Charm trigger animation.** Charms fire invisibly. Each one should flash its chip
   as it contributes, the way Balatro's jokers wiggle in sequence.
5. **Run-total anticipation.** The target bar should fill with a rising tone during
   the slam rather than jumping afterward.

## Untested

Engine is hammered; **the UI is still unclicked.** With this much new animation and
audio, expect timing problems — most likely the cascade when a hand scores zero
(no events, so it jumps straight to the slam) and audio needing a tap to unlock on
mobile Safari.
