Meldlings Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Meldlings collects no data. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

What the game stores

On your device only: your saved run, your progress (deeds, unlocked Meldlings, card backs, Pegs, your run record) and your settings (sound, text size, Reduce Motion, count speed). Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What the game doesn't do

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No network connection: the game plays fully offline.
  • Nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

Apple's privacy details

On the App Store, Meldlings is listed as "Data Not Collected". Its privacy manifest declares no tracking, no tracking domains and no collected data types. It lists four standard system features the game engine uses on your device to save and time the game (file timestamps, system boot time, disk space and app settings); none of them sends anything anywhere.

Purchases

If the game offers purchases, Apple handles payment. Meldlings never sees your payment details.

Children

Because the game collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an online feature), this page will be updated first, and the App Store listing with it.
