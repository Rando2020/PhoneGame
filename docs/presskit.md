## The short version (50 words)

Meldlings turns cribbage into a roguelite run. Keep four of six cards, throw
two to your Crib, then the cut decides. Count fifteens, pairs and runs, stack
charms that bend the math, and outscore 17 Spoilers across five acts. Free to
try on iPhone, one purchase for the rest, no ads.

## The longer version

Cribbage is a 400-year-old card game with one of the most satisfying scoring
moments in cards: the count. Meldlings builds a whole roguelite around that
moment.

Each deal gives you six cards. You keep four and throw two to your Crib, and
only then is the starter cut, so every keep is a real bet. Fifteens, pairs,
runs and flushes tick up one by one, then your multiplier slams down. Beat the
round's target in four deals and you move on.

Between rounds you spend Glim at the Peg House on charms, marked cards and
Reckonings that level up a scoring type. Every third round is a Spoiler, a boss
with a rule that breaks something you rely on. After the count comes The Play:
peg to 31 against the Spoiler, and what you peg rides your multiplier into your
score.

It's made for a phone: drag or tap, haptics on the big moments, text size and
Reduce Motion settings, and it plays fully offline.

## Features

- Cribbage scoring, counted one event at a time, then multiplied.
- 53 charms across 5 rarities (Worn, Keen, Gleaming, Hallowed, Mythic), five slots.
- 6 Meldlings to play as, each with its own trick, plus 4 ascended forms.
- 17 suit-aligned Spoilers, five drawn per run.
- 6 card marks: Sunlit, Brittle, Mimic, Offering, Minted and Fickle.
- The Play: pegging to 31 against the boss, feeding your score.
- 8 streets of difficulty, 8 trials, 17 deeds, a daily run and a Gallery.
- Free to try (act 1 and its Spoiler, every run). One unlock, no ads, no energy, nothing random for sale.
